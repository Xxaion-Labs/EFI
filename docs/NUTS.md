# NUTS — Native Universal Terminal Surface

**EFI™ · Xxaion Labs™ · PATENT PENDING**

NUTS is the replaceable, Operator-owned local embodiment surface through which a personal EFI can inhabit ordinary digital and physical reality.

## The boundary

NUTS is **not** the intelligence.

```text
personal EFI / ANEL GOOS
        ↓
      NUTS
        ↓
 computer · phone · displays · files · models · APIs · devices · sensors · world
```

The EFI performs the cognition. NUTS exposes the local machine/world surface. FAP governs the boundary when external effects are required.

## Intended experience

The target is dramatically simpler than application orchestration:

```text
open NUTS
→ authorize the personal EFI body
→ contact the EFI naturally
→ NUTS exposes permitted local/world surfaces
→ results return to the EFI
```

The person should increasingly interact with **intent + EFI + world**, not memorize the procedural grammar of every application.

## Voice-first, not voice-only

Voice is important because it can reduce the cognitive tax of translating thought into application procedure. It is not the only interface. Displays, text, touch, files, sensors, actuators, and future spatial interfaces remain available when they are the right projection for the contact.
