## Constitutional invariants

### Sovereign centers survive relation

A stronger whole must not require erasing the center that makes the relation meaningful.

### Capability does not manufacture authority

The ability to infer, compute, access a tool, or coordinate a network does not by itself create permission to act.

### Candidate is not CURRENT

Generation and admission are distinct. Claims survive through evidence appropriate to their scope.

### Reality is court

Mechanical claims require mechanical evidence. Capability claims require capability evidence. Physical and social claims require evidence at their own scale.

### Preserve rivals under underdetermination

If evidence cannot discriminate between lawful alternatives, preserve them and acquire the smallest discriminator rather than forcing a fake ranking.

### Success becomes curvature

Useful work should change retained field geometry so comparable future work becomes cheaper.

### Failure becomes immunity

A repeated failure class should become a retained invariant, regression court, or negative constraint rather than a recurring patch.
