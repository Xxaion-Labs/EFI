# EFI Commercial Licensing

EFI is public-source technology with a deliberate split:

> **People can explore and use it noncommercially. Businesses pay for commercial rights.**

## Public license

Repository software is offered for qualifying noncommercial purposes under the [EFI Public Source License](LICENSE.md), which incorporates the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0.

## Commercial use

**No commercial-use permission is granted by the public repository license.**

A separate written commercial license is required before using repository software or licensed patent rights for a commercial purpose.

Commercial licensing is intended to cover uses such as:

- internal use by a for-profit business;
- incorporation into a commercial product or service;
- SaaS, hosted access, managed services, or paid APIs;
- commercial deployment, resale, distribution, or bundling;
- consulting or professional-services deliverables;
- revenue-supporting integrations or infrastructure;
- commercial derivatives or commercial exploitation of licensed implementations.

The exact commercial scope, price, patent rights, support, warranties, redistribution rights, field of use, term, and other terms are set only by the signed commercial agreement.

## No implied deal

Viewing, forking, discussing, evaluating, or contributing through GitHub does not create a commercial license.

No commercial patent license, covenant not to sue, trademark license, exclusivity, or commercial-use immunity exists unless expressly granted in a signed written agreement by the applicable rights holder.

## Commercial licensing contact

Until a dedicated licensing channel is published, contact **Xxaion Labs through the Xxaion-Labs GitHub organization** and identify the request as **EFI COMMERCIAL LICENSE**.

## Individual and public-interest use

The project is intended to remain broadly accessible to individuals for qualifying noncommercial use. Donations do not convert an otherwise permitted noncommercial use into a commercial license.
