## What “patent pending” means here

**PATENT PENDING** means patent applications have been filed.

It does **not** mean:

- a patent has been granted;
- any particular claim will issue;
- every sentence, name, implementation detail, roadmap item, or future feature in this repository is necessarily entitled to either provisional filing date;
- publication itself creates patent ownership.

A later claim receives an earlier filing date only to the extent the earlier filed disclosure legally supports that claimed subject matter.

## Public disclosure boundary

This repository became an intentionally public EFI launch surface on **September 26, 2026**.

The public technical material is being currentized primarily inside the already-filed technical envelope. Later-developed technical details are not represented as priority-supported merely because they are adjacent to filed architecture.

Public disclosure can itself become prior art. U.S. law provides an inventor-originated disclosure grace period in circumstances defined by 35 U.S.C. 102, but other jurisdictions can apply materially different novelty rules. Publication here therefore does not substitute for a filing strategy for later-developed patentable subject matter.

The provisional applications also have their own filing-to-conversion deadlines. Public disclosure does not extend those deadlines.

Repository source-code licensing, when source is released, is a separate question from patent rights and will be stated explicitly for the relevant release.
