# EFI Brand Contract

## Identity thesis

EFI should read as a living computational field under pressure: void black, luminous constrained geometry, material structure, evidence, and continuity.

## Canonical palette

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| identity/current | Acid Green | #A8FF00 |
| relation/shared field | Electric Violet | #A855FF |
| effect/materialization | Molten Orange | #FF8A00 |
| experiment/exception | Hot Magenta | #FF00E6 |
| observation/world signal | Cyan | #00D4FF |
| substrate | Void Black | #050505 |

## Motif semantics

- Sigil ring: identity, continuity, locked center.
- Reaction ring: active transformation or high-energy state change.
- Field orbit: interacting relations or multi-center systems.
- Node: state, evidence point, source, or boundary.
- Rail/divider: transition, dependency, section boundary, or process projection.
- Star/spark: terminal accent or exceptional emphasis.
- Cracked metal: materialization, physical substrate, hard constraint.
- Plasma/glass/wave: possibility, field energy, relation, contact.

## Status vocabulary

Use visible, exact status terms: CURRENT, PROVED, OPEN, ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT, BLOCKED, NOT CLAIMED, ATTRACTOR.

## Anti-patterns

Do not use generic AI brains, blue corporate gradients, stock circuit-board wallpaper, random hex grids, neon on every word, excessive glow that reduces legibility, motif placement with no semantic job, a different logo vocabulary for each artifact, or visual claims stronger than the evidence.
