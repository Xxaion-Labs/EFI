## Claim discipline

```text
claim
+ scope
+ evidence
+ proof boundary
= publishable status
```

A successful bounded demonstration does not silently become an unrestricted capability claim. A roadmap item does not silently become CURRENT. A repository artifact does not become proof merely because it exists.
